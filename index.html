FAQ

Before we start, a few useful answers.

Do you work with companies outside Egypt?

We welcome remote projects from companies and agencies in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and across the Gulf, with delivery terms agreed for each project.

Are all your videos AI-generated?

No. We use motion graphics, animation, AI or a combination depending on the creative brief, budget and goals.

How do you price a project?

Pricing depends on duration, production style, scenes, deliverables, voice-over, revisions and usage rights. We prepare a tailored quote after reviewing your brief.

Do you offer white-label production for agencies?

Yes. We can arrange confidential production partnerships with clear scopes and brand guidelines.

Can you deliver different aspect ratios?

Yes, platform-specific versions can be included in the agreed scope.
