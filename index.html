FAQ

Clear scope. Clear answers.

A few details before we build your proposal.

Do you charge by the hour?

Our proposals are based on monthly packages or complete production scopes. Fees reflect the agreed deliverables, creative complexity, reviews and usage rights.

Can we sign a six- or twelve-month partnership?

Yes. We can propose a six- or twelve-month engagement with defined monthly production capacity, delivery planning and agreed monthly fees.

Which markets do you work with?

We collaborate remotely with companies and agencies in Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and Egypt. We agree the language, target audience and deliverable formats before production.

Are quantities and revisions unlimited?

The package lists its base deliverables. Two consolidated review rounds are included per batch or project after concept approval. Additional quantities, languages or a change in scope are agreed separately before production.

Do packages include advertising spend?

Packages cover the agreed creative production. Media spend and any additional filming or third-party licences are specified separately when relevant.
