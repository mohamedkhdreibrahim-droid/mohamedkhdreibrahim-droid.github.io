١. إعلان منتج — مناسب لبرطمان أو عبوة
Animate the provided [product] image. The product rises from the bottom and makes one slow quarter turn. Keep the shape and label consistent. End on the front-facing label, held still for one second. Fixed camera, vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.

٢. تحريك شخصية مرسومة
Animate the illustrated [character]. The character looks up, blinks once, and smiles gently. Preserve the face, clothes and colors. Keep the background and camera still. End on the smile. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.

٣. عمق في صورة
Create a slow camera push-in on the provided [scene]. The foreground moves slightly faster than the background. Preserve the composition and lighting. No new objects or cuts. End on [focal point]. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.
