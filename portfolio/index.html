FAQ

Before you ask…

Is it really free? What’s the catch?

Really free. I’m building a portfolio and an audience, and every project is proof I can show. All I ask is permission to show the work (and if you’d rather not, just say so).

How fast will I get it?

A reel, script or voice-over is usually delivered within 48 hours of agreeing on the plan, and your first message gets a reply within 24 hours.

What’s the difference between motion graphics and AI video?

Motion graphics are designed titles, shapes and icons in motion; AI generates realistic or cartoon scenes and shots. The best work combines both, and that’s my specialty.

Who owns the work?

You do. The final files are yours to use as you like, and I only use licensed or royalty-free voices and music.

Can the voice-over be in my own voice?

Yes. Record one clean minute and I create a clone of your voice that can read any script, which keeps your content truly yours.
